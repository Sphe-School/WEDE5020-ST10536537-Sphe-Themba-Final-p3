About Big Tech Solutions

Big Tech Solutions is a small technology company that provides affordable IT services to businesses and individuals.

The company provides computer repairs, computer parts, website development, network installation and technical support

Our History

Big Tech Solutions was founded in 2025 by three friends who wanted to help local businesses with affordable IT services.

The company offers computer repairs and sells computer parts such as RAM and graphics cards.

The company also provides website development, network installation and technical support.

Our Mission

To provide reliable and affordable technology services to businesses and individuals.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's most trusted local IT companies.

Our Target Audience

Our target audience includes:

  • Small Businesses
  • Students
  • Schools
  • Home Users

Why Does the Company Need a Website?

The website will help Big Tech Solutions get more customers and show customers the services that the company provides.

Customers will also be able to book repairs, display prices and contact the business.

Current Website Situation

Big Tech Solutions currently relies on Facebook and WhatsApp for customers.

The company does not have a professional website. This makes it difficult for potential customers to view services or contact the company online.

Website Goal

A professional website will improve credibility, attract more customers and make it easier for clients to request services.
